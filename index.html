How to play

There's one mystery emoji every day. Ask yes/no questions to narrow it down, then guess it.

  • ask() — type a yes/no question or tap one from the question bank. You get 20 questions.
  • guess() — name the emoji. You have 5 lives; each wrong guess costs one.
  • Typing “Is it a banana?” in ask mode counts as a guess.
  • Hints (💡) — 3 per game: a free clue, the first letter, then a blurry peek.
  • Tap a bank tile once to pick it, again (or Submit) to ask. Shuffle deals new questions.
